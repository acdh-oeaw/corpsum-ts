# NoSketch engines

This page lists the available instances. Their corpora provide the data for CorpSum visualizations. To add an instance, click ![NoSketch engines](/assets/documentation/de/image12.png), enter the required information in the form, and click **Create**.

Once you have obtained and configured access to the amc, you will see the following:

![NoSketch engines](/assets/documentation/de/image13.png)

The card shows whether you have basic access (**basic**) and whether your credentials are configured (**Configured**), along with other details about the instance. Four icons at the bottom provide additional options. The lock opens credential management. The pencil edits the instance details and is grayed out when editing is unavailable. The trash icon deletes the instance and is grayed out when deletion is unavailable. The eye opens the instance details, including its list of available corpora.
