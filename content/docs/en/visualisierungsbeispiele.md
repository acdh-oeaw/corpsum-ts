# Visualization examples

The examples below use *Paradeiser* and *Tomate*, two German words for tomato, in the amc_5.25q4LTS corpus with a lemmarow query.

## Frequencies over time

![Frequencies over time](/assets/documentation/de/image23.png)

This chart shows how query results are distributed across years. Use the percent (%) and number (#) buttons to switch between relative and absolute values. Click ![Frequencies over time](/assets/documentation/de/image24.png) to open the data table behind the chart. The ![Frequencies over time](/assets/documentation/de/image25.png) icon shows when the corpus was queried. If needed, click ![Frequencies over time](/assets/documentation/de/image26.png) to refresh the query. Hover over a data point for more information, such as the frequency for that year. Hover details are available throughout CorpSum visualizations.

## Frequencies by time interval

![Frequencies by time interval](/assets/documentation/de/image27.png)

This chart groups query results into intervals of two years in the example shown. You can set the interval to between two and ten years. Use ![Frequencies by time interval](/assets/documentation/de/image28.png) to group intervals from the beginning of the selected observation period (left button) or its end (right button).

## Collocations

![Collocations](/assets/documentation/de/image29.png)

![Collocations](/assets/documentation/de/image30.png)

For each search term, this visualization shows the words that most frequently occur with it. Use ![Collocations](/assets/documentation/de/image31.png) to choose logDice (left button) or collocation frequency (right button) as the association measure (weight). Further visualization settings are available under ![Collocations](/assets/documentation/de/image32.png).

## Media sources

![Media sources](/assets/documentation/de/image33.png)

This visualization shows query frequencies by media source. The amc provides a [complete list of media sources](https://amc.acdh.oeaw.ac.at/dokumentation/medienliste/). Use ![Media sources](/assets/documentation/de/image34.png) to sort sources from lowest to highest frequency (left button) or highest to lowest (right button). Use ![Media sources](/assets/documentation/de/image35.png) to switch between separate bars (left), stacked bars (middle), and percentage bars (right). Stacked bars are the default and are shown here. You can choose absolute (#) or relative (%) frequencies and limit the chart to between 5 and 30 sources.

## Media types

![Media types](/assets/documentation/de/image36.png)

This chart shows how matches are distributed by [media type](https://amc.acdh.oeaw.ac.at/dokumentation/korpusinhalt-attribute/). It offers the same options as the media sources chart and also shows the distribution in pie charts. You can display absolute (#) or relative (%) frequencies.

## Regional frequencies

![Regional frequencies](/assets/documentation/de/image37.png)

This visualization shows query frequencies across Austrian regions as defined by the amc. Use ![Regional frequencies](/assets/documentation/de/image38.png) to switch between a combined map (left, shown here), which plots the queries on one map and uses pie charts to show their proportions in each region, and separate maps (right), which show each query individually. You can display absolute (#) or relative (%) frequencies.

![Regional frequencies](/assets/documentation/de/image39.png)

This view also shows results as bar charts for each region or subregion. As with media sources, use ![Regional frequencies](/assets/documentation/de/image35.png) to choose the bar chart type.

## Word form frequencies

![Word form frequencies](/assets/documentation/de/image40.png)

![Word form frequencies](/assets/documentation/de/image41.png)

This visualization shows the word forms returned by the query. Use ![Word form frequencies](/assets/documentation/de/image42.png) to sort them by frequency in ascending or descending order. You can switch between absolute (#) and relative (%) frequencies and display between 5 and 30 rows.

## Keyword in Context (KWIC)

![Keyword in Context (KWIC)](/assets/documentation/de/image43.png)

This view shows matches with the surrounding text. Click ![Keyword in Context (KWIC)](/assets/documentation/de/image44.png) to display metadata annotations. Select specific options using ![Keyword in Context (KWIC)](/assets/documentation/de/image45.png) to refine the display. The AMC NoSketch Engine offers more ways to inspect individual matches.

## Options menu

Click the three dots at the upper right of a visualization to open its options menu: ![Options menu](/assets/documentation/de/image46.png). From there, you can open the chart in full screen or export the chart or its underlying data.
